Model an online store with catalog items described by name, description, price, brand, type, and image. Administrators can create, edit, and delete catalog items; shoppers can browse and filter the catalog.

Shoppers add items to a basket, change quantities, and check out when signed in. When an anonymous shopper signs in, their basket is merged into the signed-in shopper's basket. Existing shopper prices take precedence during a merge.

Checkout creates an order for the signed-in shopper and records a shipping address plus a snapshot of each purchased catalog item's name and image and each basket line's price and quantity. Later catalog changes do not rewrite the order. An empty basket cannot be checked out. Authorization is enforced by the web framework; persistence, concurrent requests, and failure recovery are outside this model.
